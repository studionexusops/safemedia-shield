## 3. App Store Listing Detailed Description

### English Version (for Shopify App Store Global)

```markdown
# Protect Your Google Shopping Revenue Without Sacrificing Storefront Sales

Google Merchant Center strictly prohibits promotional overlays—such as **"50% OFF" badges, watermarks, free shipping ribbons, and sales text**—on primary product images. Policy violations lead to item disapprovals and advertising interruptions.

However, stripping promotional badges from your online store tanks your conversion rate. Merchandising badges drive urgency and sales.

**AdFeed Clean: Google Shopping solves this dilemma with automated Dual-Feed routing.**

---

### How Dual-Feed Smart Routing Works

1. **Upload As Usual:** Upload your promotional product photos to Shopify Admin as you normally do.
2. **Automatic AI Inpainting:** Our event-driven pipeline automatically detects badges, text, and watermarks, inpainting a pristine, policy-compliant primary image in seconds.
3. **Private Metafield Storage:** The clean image is saved to a dedicated Shopify product metafield (`safemedia.clean_ad_image_url`). Your storefront theme continues showing your original sales badges.
4. **Google Shopping Integration:** Point your Google & YouTube channel or preferred feed management tool to the metafield image URL. Google receives clean images designed for editorial policy compliance; your shoppers get high-converting promo graphics.

---

### Built with Reliable Store Protection

* **Non-Destructive Routing:** Original master images and theme files are never overwritten.
* **Variant Mapping Protection:** Multi-variant products (size, color, material) are verified before and after generation to ensure variant selectors never disconnect.
* **1-Click Snapshot Restore:** Immutable point-in-time cloud snapshots enable seamless catalog restoration whenever needed.
* **5-Product Free Canary Test:** Test the AI cleanup and variant preservation on a sample product before committing.

---

### Pricing & Trial

* **$65.00 USD / month** (Flat-rate transparent subscription).
* **14-Day Free Trial** on initial installation.
* **5 Sample Preview Tests** included free.
* No long-term commitments. Cancel anytime from your Shopify Admin.
```
